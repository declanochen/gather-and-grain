Our story

Why "Gather & Grain"

Gather & Grain started the way most good things do — in our own kitchen, with a starter we've kept alive for years.

Every loaf is homemade and baked in small batches, never rushed. We use organic flour, filtered water, sea salt, and ingredients we can actually pronounce.

The "Gather" is for the garden out back — the herbs, the garlic, whatever's ready to pick that week. The "Grain" is for the sourdough itself, the part of this that's been with us the longest.

We're a small, local bakery in Media, PA, and every order is made with care — for neighbors, not strangers.

"Homemade, local, and made with care — that's the whole recipe."
